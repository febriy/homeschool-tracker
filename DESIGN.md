# Wonder and Worship design

The parent approved the Citrus & quiet wonder concept board and requested it be implemented. The visual reference is pale powder-blue watercolour paper, luminous golden citrus, olive-green leaves, white blossoms, and a delicate cream butterfly. The previous books, teacup, plum, and morning-glory identity is replaced.

Use warm ivory (#faf6ed), near-white cream working surfaces (#fffdf6), warm brown ink (#493b29), muted olive-brown text (#6d634e), olive actions (#59662d), soft warm borders (#ded5c1), and pale blue day panels (#e5edec). Selected learner pills are olive with white text. Navigation uses clear small outlined icons and labels, with a quiet pale-olive active state. Resource links remain plain underlined text without chip borders or background.

The day heading is centered above its real date and an immersive full-width citrus border. Use the original watercolour paper image as the panel background. Keep the center clear and all illustrations out of text and controls. The citrus branch appears in the header, secondary page headings and photo empty state. Allura is self-hosted for the complete homeschool name; Fraunces remains the readable heading face, and system sans serif remains for controls and detailed body text. The day introduction and date use a quiet readable serif, matching the approved preview.

Keep the selected child visible above every view. Child buttons use names and initials; colour alone never communicates ownership. Lessons, skills, and photos belong to the child explicitly selected in their forms. Existing together records remain accessible. Family Bible and loop activities remain shared.

Photos have dates, subjects, and optional captions. Resize uploads in the browser, retain selections after failed requests, and make removal recoverable. The app has no sign-in: its copy must clearly say that visitors to the tracker can view uploaded photos. Blob credentials stay on the server.

Phone layouts use one-column forms and a persistent navigation bar. All controls need visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Avoid adding decorative cards around every row.

Progress uses checkbox selections from the same subject lists as Skills. Saving a lesson records child, date, and subject; photos share that context. Review counts distinct subject days and shows an editable attendance sheet by week or month. Literary Ladder preserves the supplied CMCC worksheet data with independent per-child reading ticks. Literature offers ten optional daily book titles.

The installed icon uses the two-fruit citrus branch on pale-blue watercolour paper above equal-size, Allura-outlined “Wonder” and “and Worship” lines in warm brown. Standard, Apple touch, and padded maskable PNG exports share the same composition; the favicon uses the branch alone.

Learner profiles include a permanent adult “My learning” tab (stable ID parent) alongside individual children and Children together. The adult curriculum and loop position are independent. Today, Progress, Review, Photos, and Study plan follow the learner; child Skills and Literary Ladder navigation is hidden for the adult curriculum. Adult Mandarin is a daily podcast, 联合早报 article, and Preply vocabulary checklist. Adult Literature offers its two named books plus three optional daily titles; children retain ten. History offers all learners five optional country-book titles and an optional country/region, saved by learner and date and included in History review. Learner management supports renaming the adult profile without changing its curriculum identity.

Resource links are plain underlined text with no border or chip background, enough line height, and 44px touch targets. Without Doors has no outbound resource link in either learner curriculum.

Play adapts the family’s Stories of Play planner into optional weekly notice/plan/observe/reflect notes, child interest profiles, play ideas and materials rotation, age-filtered growth observations, monthly reflections and a shared family purpose. English and Chinese are complete. Child records and drafts stay separate; Together remains its own journal. Only explicitly checked play-learning dates contribute to Review as Life & learning. Plans and growth observations do not mark attendance or formal skill mastery. Weekly/monthly photos reuse Life & learning photo records and preserve learner/date context. The source PDF is not distributed with the app. Play remains visible for all learners; from the adult profile it offers a child selector before opening their planner. Adult attendance includes English while the adult lesson curriculum remains unchanged.
