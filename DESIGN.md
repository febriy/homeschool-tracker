# Wonder and Worship design

The parent requested bright orange and yellow, with a new identity expressing hope, wonder, joy, and peace. The app is used to record children’s learning separately, often on a phone.

Use warm cream paper (#fffaf0), golden yellow and pale saffron for Today and Bible activities, terracotta actions (#a63f13), sage green for covered skills (#405d39), dark brown text (#382716), and warm muted text (#6e5a40). The parent’s supplied watercolour references establish a domestic botanical journal: everyday ceramics, books, fruit, flowers, and garden wildlife. Original transparent illustrations use golden flowers, peach petals, pale blue china, yellow lemons, sage foliage, and a songbird on well-loved books. Preserve their natural contours and translucent washes; do not substitute geometric flowers or flat vector scenes.

Today carries the table still life beside its real daily learning content. Books and the garden bird accompany Progress, Skills, Review, and Literary Ladder. Other page headings and the photo empty state use small related illustrations. On phones, keep art clear of text and buttons and preserve visible curriculum tasks in the first screen. Forms, attendance tables, skill checkboxes, and uploaded photos remain on plain readable surfaces. Retain the small First Light brand mark and calligraphic name as continuity; hope, wonder, joy, and peace now also live in nature and ordinary home life.

The complete homeschool name uses one self-hosted, softly calligraphic Courgette face at one weight. Both lines in the app icon use the same font and size, converted to outlines for reliable export. Fraunces remains the readable heading face and the device sans serif carries controls and body copy.

Keep the selected child visible above every view. Child buttons use names and initials; colour alone never communicates ownership. Lessons, skills, and photos belong to the child explicitly selected in their forms. Existing together records remain accessible. Family Bible and loop activities remain shared.

Photos have dates, subjects, and optional captions. Resize uploads in the browser, retain selections after failed requests, and make removal recoverable. The app has no sign-in: its copy must clearly say that visitors to the tracker can view uploaded photos. Blob credentials stay on the server.

Phone layouts use one-column forms and a persistent navigation bar. All controls need visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Avoid adding decorative cards around every row.

Progress uses checkbox selections from the same subject lists as Skills. Saving a lesson records child, date, and subject; photos share that context. Review counts distinct subject days and shows an editable attendance sheet by week or month. Literary Ladder preserves the supplied CMCC worksheet data with independent per-child reading ticks. Literature offers ten optional daily book titles.

The installed app icon uses the same First Light symbol and the name “Wonder and Worship” on warm cream. Source: editable app-icon.svg. Export Apple touch, standard web app, and padded maskable PNG icons. At favicon size use the sunrise mark alone for recognition.
